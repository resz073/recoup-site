Recoup

Get back every taka you spend for the office or lend to people.

An Android app, locked with your PIN

What Recoup does

Office bills, until you're paid back

Note what you spend for the office, then follow each bill from "to bill" through "in billing" to complete. Bills waiting more than 30 days are flagged.

Who owes you, and who you owe

Keep money you lend or borrow, part payments and due dates, and share a statement with the other person.

Locked and encrypted

Recoup opens with your PIN or fingerprint. Your ledger is encrypted on the phone and in every backup, and a recovery key gets you back in if you forget the PIN.

A copy in your own Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, an encrypted copy is kept in Recoup's hidden folder in your Drive, synced across your phones, with a daily copy for the last 14 days.

Your records stay yours

Recoup has no server, no ads and no tracking. Your ledger stays on your phone, and the copy in Google Drive is encrypted with your PIN before it leaves the phone, so nobody else can read it, including the developer.
